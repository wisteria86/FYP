# Runtime acceptance worksheet

Record date, build/commit, model/provider, speech engines, browser, audio device, reviewer and language. Use fictional information. The following work requires people and physical devices; no result is pre-filled as passed.

## Audio and turn-taking

Run greetings, appointments, a three-person meeting and negotiation using both a headset and laptop speakers. Test at least ten learner interruptions per device, including quiet speech, short hesitation, room noise and the AI's own playback.

Record first-audio latency, interruption-to-silence latency, false interruptions, lost initial words, overlapping AI voices, stale playback, and whether the next response refers only to speech actually heard. Confirm manual Interrupt remains usable. Check pause/resume and reconnect during synthesis.

## English and Urdu reviewers

Rate each language separately. For each lesson sample include an appropriate paraphrase, a negation, an irrelevant keyword-rich answer, a quoted/copied model, a clarification request and a corrected recognition error.

Record the learner evidence, expected objective outcome, system outcome, appropriateness of role/register, factual consistency, correction usefulness and any disagreement. Reviewers must not infer emotion, personality or pronunciation from transcripts. Urdu reviewers should check meaning, directionality, comprehensibility and natural phrasing.

## Negotiation reviewers

Test a feasible package, an infeasible package, conditional concessions, an unauthorized approver, a justified walk-away, an unsupported benchmark and a confidentiality breach. Compare ledger transitions with quoted dialogue. Rate decision quality separately from English quality. Verify private learner preparation never appears in the counterpart's prompt or speech without learner disclosure.

## Learning follow-up

Compare supported performance with a fresh independent case, then repeat the skill at the scheduled review dates. Record retained/transfer evidence, learner reflection, perceived usefulness and whether feedback enabled a meaningful improvement. Have qualified human raters review disagreements before making effectiveness or proficiency claims.
